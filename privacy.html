Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

OmniUPI (“OmniUPI”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides UPI payment gateway and payment infrastructure services for businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and how users can contact us.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as your name, email address, business details, login information, and information you provide while using OmniUPI. When you use payment or merchant features, we may process transaction-related information needed to provide those services.

2. Google Sign-In

OmniUPI may use Google Sign-In to authenticate merchant accounts. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides information permitted by the OAuth consent screen, such as your email address and basic profile information. We use this information to authenticate and create or access your OmniUPI merchant account. We do not use Google account data for advertising.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide payment and merchant features, verify transactions, provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain our systems, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Payment Information

OmniUPI processes payment-related information required for merchant payment operations. Sensitive payment credentials such as UPI PINs are not requested by OmniUPI. Users should never share UPI PINs, OTPs, or banking passwords with OmniUPI or any person claiming to represent it.

5. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers and infrastructure partners only when necessary to operate, secure, or support the service, or when required by law. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect account and transaction information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal or regulatory requirements.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to request information about your account data or ask questions about privacy. Some information may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal compliance.

9. Children

OmniUPI is intended for businesses and is not directed to children.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact omniupi@gmail.com.
