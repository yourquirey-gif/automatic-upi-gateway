Refund Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This policy explains cancellation and refund handling for OmniUPI subscription plans and service payments.

1. Subscription Payments

Subscription plans are activated for the period shown at checkout. Because access to digital services may begin immediately, refund eligibility depends on the circumstances of the request and the portion of the service already used.

2. Refund Requests

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact OmniUPI with your registered email address, transaction details, and reason for the request. We will review the request and communicate the outcome.

3. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

Confirmed duplicate charges or demonstrably incorrect charges may be eligible for correction or refund after verification.

4. Payment Disputes

For a payment that appears pending or unsuccessful, allow the payment system to complete its status update before requesting a refund. Do not share UPI PINs or OTPs with anyone.

5. Contact

For refund and billing questions, contact omniupi@gmail.com.
