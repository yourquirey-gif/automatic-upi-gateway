Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective: 18 August 2026 · Last updated: 18 August 2026

OmniUPI primarily provides digital software, merchant tools and online services. No physical shipment is normally required for these services.

Digital delivery: Eligible features are delivered electronically through the OmniUPI website, merchant dashboard, API or another configured online channel.

1. Digital Delivery

After successful account creation or subscription activation, access to eligible digital features is provided electronically through the applicable service interface.

2. Activation

Activation may be immediate or may require account verification, payment confirmation, merchant approval or other checks depending on the service configuration.

3. No Physical Shipping

The standard OmniUPI service does not require courier delivery, shipping charges or a physical delivery address. If a future service includes a physical item, separate delivery terms will be shown.

4. Service Delays

Third-party outages, verification queues, payment confirmation delays or maintenance may delay activation. We will take reasonable steps to restore or complete delivery of the digital service.

5. Contact

For questions about digital delivery or activation, use the Contact Us option on OmniUPI or the support facility available in the merchant dashboard.
