OmniUPI Privacy Policy

Effective: 17 August 2026 · Last updated: 17 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OmniUPI collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the OmniUPI website, merchant dashboard, payment-link features and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the feature you use, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, login details, merchant identifiers, UPI IDs, transaction or payment-link information, device and browser information, IP address, logs and support communications.

2. Google Sign-In and Google User Data

OmniUPI uses Google Sign-In to authenticate merchant accounts. When you choose Google Sign-In, we may receive basic Google account information made available through the configured Google authentication scopes, such as your name, email address and profile information when provided by Google. We use this information to authenticate you, create or access your OmniUPI merchant account, maintain account security and provide the requested merchant service.

OmniUPI does not sell Google user data and does not use Google user data for targeted advertising, personalized advertising, credit scoring, lending, data brokerage or training generalized AI/ML models. Google user data is not transferred to third parties except service providers or integrations that are reasonably necessary to provide, secure or operate the requested OmniUPI functionality, or when disclosure is required by law.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, provide merchant and payment-link functionality, authenticate users, facilitate configured payment workflows, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, provide support, troubleshoot issues, improve the platform and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Payment Information

OmniUPI should not be used to collect or store card PINs, UPI PINs, CVV values, banking-app passwords or other authentication secrets. Payment authentication may be handled by a bank, UPI participant, payment provider or merchant application under its own applicable terms.

5. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be shared with vendors and service providers that help operate hosting, databases, authentication, communication, security, analytics or payment-related integrations, only to the extent reasonably necessary. We may disclose information when required by law or to investigate fraud, security incidents or abuse.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information, including access controls, authentication, encrypted connections and logging where applicable. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should protect passwords, API credentials and devices.

7. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, security, dispute handling, backups, audits and applicable legal obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information where applicable, subject to legal, security, accounting and transaction-record requirements. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use essential cookies or local storage for login sessions, preferences, security and functionality. Optional analytics or similar technologies, if enabled, may be described in the relevant service interface.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy, account or security questions, use the Support Ticket feature in the OmniUPI merchant dashboard where enabled.
